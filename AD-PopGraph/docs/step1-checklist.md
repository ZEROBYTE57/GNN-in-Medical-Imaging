# Step 1 checklist
- [ ] Apply for ADNI access (adni.loni.usc.edu -> Data -> Apply for access). Use your institutional email and supervisor details.
- [ ] Ask supervisor to sign off / be listed as required by the application.
- [ ] After approval: download ADNIMERGE and the regional MRI (FreeSurfer) and FDG-PET tables; note which exist.
- [ ] Record the subject counts per group (AD, NC, sMCI, pMCI) with both modalities.
